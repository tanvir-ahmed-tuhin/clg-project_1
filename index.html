About CSE Department

Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) is one of the most important and rapidly growing fields of technology and innovation. It combines computer science, programming, mathematics, and engineering to create modern solutions for real-world problems. CSE plays a major role in shaping the future of technology and has become an essential part of our everyday lives.
